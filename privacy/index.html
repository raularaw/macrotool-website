Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Who we are

macrotool ("macrotool", "we", "us") is an independent software developer operated by Raul Araw. We publish add-ons for Google Workspace, including Tab Transfer for Google Sheets (together, the "Apps"). This policy explains what information the Apps access, how it is used, and the choices you have. Contact: support@macrotool.app.

The short version

  • The Apps run inside Google Apps Script, under your own Google account.
  • We do not operate servers that receive, copy or store your spreadsheet content or email.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Settings are stored inside your own Google file or Google account.

Information the Apps access

Tab Transfer

When you use Tab Transfer in a spreadsheet, it accesses, through Google's APIs:

  • Spreadsheet content in the spreadsheet where you open it — tab names, header names, cell values, formulas, formatting, notes and data validation — so it can move rows between tabs as you configure.
  • Your Google account email address — to identify the automation owner, to show who saved the setup, and to send test emails to you.
  • Email recipient addresses and message content you configure, including addresses read from a column you choose — only to send the notifications you set up.
  • Your email signature, if you paste one into the app.

How the information is used

Information is used solely to provide the features you see in the Apps: reading statuses, moving rows, applying formatting, creating the triggers that run your automation, recording an activity log and sending the emails you configure. It is not used for any other purpose.

Where information is stored

  • Setup and activity log — stored with the spreadsheet itself (Apps Script document properties and spreadsheet developer metadata), visible to the add-on in that spreadsheet only.
  • Email signature — stored in your Apps Script user properties, tied to your Google account.
  • Emails — sent through Google's MailApp service from your Google account; copies may appear in your Gmail Sent folder according to Google's behaviour.

All of this stays within Google's infrastructure, protected by Google's security. macrotool does not have access to your spreadsheets or stored settings.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent or transfer your information to third parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising in the Apps. The only party that processes your data is Google, as the provider of Google Workspace and Apps Script, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

macrotool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular: we only use Google user data to provide and improve user-facing features of the Apps; we do not transfer it except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you; we do not use it for advertising; and no human reads it unless you give explicit permission (for example when you share a spreadsheet with us for support), it is needed for security purposes, or it is required by law.

Retention and deletion

Settings and the activity log remain in your spreadsheet until you remove them or delete the file. The activity log keeps only the 25 most recent runs. To remove access entirely, uninstall the add-on (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons) and revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Any triggers the add-on created stop when access is revoked.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it no longer than needed for that purpose.

This website

macrotool.app is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information such as your IP address. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The Apps are business tools and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact
