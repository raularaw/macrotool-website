Refund Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

We want you to be happy with every macrotool add-on you buy.

14-day refund, no questions asked

If Tab Transfer Pro, Tab Transfer Ultra or the Pro to Ultra upgrade isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Email support@macrotool.app from any address with the email of the Google account you bought it for, or reply to your Paddle receipt. You don't need to give a reason.

How refunds are paid

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method, including any tax you paid. Depending on your bank, the money can take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

What happens to your plan

A refunded purchase returns your account to the plan it had before: a refunded upgrade goes back to Pro, and a refunded Pro or Ultra purchase goes back to the free plan. Your Tab Transfer settings are kept.

If we ever discontinue Tab Transfer

We will give at least 90 days' notice, and anyone who bought a license in the 12 months before the announcement may request a full refund.

Questions

Email support@macrotool.app. For questions about a charge, you can also contact Paddle through paddle.net. See also our Terms of Service.
