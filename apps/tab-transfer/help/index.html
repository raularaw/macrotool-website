Tab Transfer guide

How every part of Tab Transfer works. In the add-on, the small ? next to a heading opens the matching section here.

1. Install

  1. Open the Tab Transfer listing on the Google Workspace Marketplace and click Install. (The listing link appears on the Tab Transfer page once it's published.)
  2. Choose the Google account you use for Sheets, review the permissions and click Allow. Each permission is explained on the Tab Transfer page.

Using Google Workspace at work or school? Your administrator may need to allow the add-on, or can install it for the whole organisation from the Admin console.

2. Open Tab Transfer

In any spreadsheet you can edit, open the Extensions → Tab Transfer menu:

  • Open Tab Transfer: opens the sidebar on the right. It has five tabs: Move, Rules, Behavior, Email and Log.
  • Email & signature: opens the wide email editor (owner only, after the setup is saved).
  • Help & guide: this page.
  • Upgrade / license: shows your license, or the upgrade options on the Free plan.
  • Contact support: email us at support@macrotool.app.

If the menu doesn't appear right after installing, reload the spreadsheet tab.

3. Prepare your sheet

  • Headers go in row 1 of every tab. Tab Transfer reads row 1 as the column names.
  • Use the same status column name (for example Status) on every tab rows should move out of, and on destination tabs too if rows may move on again later.
  • Create the destination tabs first, for example Approved, Rejected, Archive.
  • Columns are matched by header name (ignoring upper/lower case), not position. Destination tabs can use a different column order, skip columns, or add columns of their own.

Tip: try your first setup on a copy of the spreadsheet (File → Make a copy).

4. Rules

The Rules tab decides which rows move and where. Changes apply after you press Save setup at the bottom of the sidebar. The first person to save becomes the automation owner.

Status column

Pick the header whose value decides where a row goes. The list shows the headers found in row 1 of your tabs.

Watched tabs

Rows are only moved out of checked tabs. Uncheck tabs that should never be scanned, such as a dashboard or an archive. Tabs without the status column are ignored automatically.

Statuses & destinations

  • Type a status and press Enter, then choose its destination tab. Matching ignores upper/lower case and extra spaces, so approved and Approved are the same status.
  • Import from saves typing: status dropdown reads the dropdown options of the status column, selected cells uses the cells you've selected in the sheet, and selected cell's dropdown uses the dropdown of the selected cell.
  • Auto-match tab names sends each status to the tab with the same name.
  • A status without a destination is ignored. Rows with it stay where they are.
  • The Free plan includes 3 rules; the lifetime license removes the limit.

5. Behavior

Timestamp column

Optional. Choose a header (for example Status Timestamp) and Tab Transfer fills it with the date and time when a status changes or a row moves, on the source tab and on the destination.

After a row moves

  • Delete it from the source tab (default). Moved rows are removed in grouped blocks, which is fast even for thousands of rows. If every data row leaves a tab, row 2 is cleared instead of deleted so its formulas and checkboxes survive as a template.
  • Clear it, keep the row. Values are cleared; formulas stay and checkboxes are unticked.

Formatting of moved rows

  • Keep the row's own formatting: fill, font, colours, number formats, alignment and wrapping travel with each cell. Colours from conditional formatting are never copied, because the destination's own rules should decide those.
  • Match the destination tab: new rows copy the look of the destination's last row.
  • Don't change formatting: only values move.

Links inside cells and HYPERLINK() formulas always stay clickable.

6. Automation

  • Move instantly: as soon as anyone changes a status, editors included, that row moves. It runs under the owner's account.
  • Run on a schedule (lifetime license): every 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 12 hours, or daily at a chosen hour in the spreadsheet's time zone. Use it when statuses are set by formulas, Google Forms or imports, which don't count as edits.

Google allows add-on schedules at most once an hour. Turning an option off removes its trigger when you save.

7. Move tab

The Move tab is the home screen.

  • Plan card: your plan, with Upgrade and Already bought? (re-checks your license).
  • This tab: moves every row on the tab you're viewing whose status has a destination. It follows the tab you click in the sheet.
  • All tabs: the same for every watched tab.

Both buttons stay greyed out until the setup is saved.

Status

Shows who owns the automation, whether instant moves and the schedule are on, and the email setup at a glance.

8. Formulas, checkboxes & formatting

  • Formulas in the moved row arrive as their calculated values, because their cell references wouldn't be correct on another tab. HYPERLINK("…","…") formulas are kept as live formulas.
  • Per-row formulas in the destination (for example =C2*D2 in every row, including rows pre-filled below the data) are extended to the new rows automatically.
  • Formulas that fill a whole column are left alone: an ARRAYFORMULA, BYROW/MAP, QUERY or FILTER in row 2, or a formula in the header cell such as ={"Total";ARRAYFORMULA(…)}. Tab Transfer writes around them.
  • A destination column with no formula receives the value from the source, like a manual entry.
  • Checkboxes keep their ticked or unticked state, including custom values such as Yes/No. Empty pre-filled checkboxes below your data don't count as data, so new rows fill those rows first.
  • Dropdowns keep their style (chips stay chips). Notes and links come along.

9. Email notifications

Lifetime license. In the sidebar's Email tab, click Open email & signature editor, switch email on and add notifications. For each one choose:

  • When rows move to: a specific tab, or any tab.
  • Send: An email per row, or One summary per run listing every moved row.
  • To / CC: fixed addresses, and/or Also email the address in column to use each row's own address (for example the Email column).
  • Subject and message: click a placeholder to insert it: any header as {{Header Name}}, plus {{From Tab}}, {{To Tab}}, {{Row Count}}, {{Moved At}}, {{Spreadsheet Name}} and {{Spreadsheet Link}}. Checkboxes appear as ☑ / ☐.
  • Include row details adds a table of the moved row(s). Add my signature is set per notification.

Attachments

Click Attach files to add files from your computer: up to 5 files, 15 MB each and 20 MB in total. They're saved in a Tab Transfer attachments folder in your own Google Drive and attached to every email the notification sends. Tab Transfer can only see the files it stored there, never the rest of your Drive. If you delete a file, emails go out without it and the Log tells you.

Preview and test

Preview email shows the exact email using a real row; Send me a test emails it to you, attachments included. Press Save email settings to apply.

Emails are sent from the owner's Google account and count toward Google's daily sending quota (about 100 recipients a day for free Gmail accounts, more on Google Workspace). If the quota runs out, the remaining emails are skipped and the Log says so.

10. Sender & signature

  • Sender name and Reply-to address are optional.
  • Signature: open Gmail → Settings → Signature, select your signature, copy, and paste it into the editor. You can also paste HTML from a signature generator, or edit the HTML directly. The preview shows exactly how it will look.
  • Use images hosted on the web (https links); pasted or embedded images are removed because email apps block them. Scripts and unsafe code are stripped automatically.
  • If spacing around a heading looks too big, the signature's HTML is probably missing style="margin:0" on that heading or paragraph.

11. Activity log

The Log tab lists the 25 most recent runs: when they ran, how they were triggered (Instant, Manual, Scheduled or Continued), how many rows went to each tab, emails sent, and any problems. The Move tab shows the latest three.

12. Plans & license

  • Free, forever: 3 status rules, instant and manual moves, and everything in section 8.
  • Lifetime license: $18 once. Unlimited rules, email notifications with your signature and attachments, and scheduled runs, on every spreadsheet you automate. No subscription.

Upgrade from the sidebar (Upgrade on the Move tab) or from Extensions → Tab Transfer → Upgrade / license. Payment is handled by Stripe; your license activates on its own within a few seconds. Bought already but still see Free? Click Already bought? on the Move tab. The license belongs to the Google account you bought it with. 14-day refund: see the terms.

13. Owners & editors

  • The first person to save the setup becomes the owner. Only the owner can change the setup, run batch moves and edit emails. Only the owner needs a license.
  • Editors change statuses as usual; the owner's automation moves their rows and sends emails on the owner's behalf. When an editor opens Tab Transfer they see who the owner is and where each status goes.
  • If the owner loses access to the file, an editor can take over. The previous owner is notified by email.

14. Large sheets & limits

Google stops any add-on run after 6 minutes. For very large batches Tab Transfer works to a budget: it stops starting new moves at 5 minutes and stops sending emails at 5½ minutes, then schedules itself to continue about a minute later, repeating until everything is done. A tab is never half-moved, and unsent emails are queued and sent first by the next run, so nothing is duplicated or lost. You'll see Continued entries in the Log.

15. Troubleshooting

Rows aren't moving

  • Check the status column header is in row 1 and spelled the same on each tab.
  • Check the status has a destination in Rules and the tab still exists.
  • Make sure the tab is checked under Watched tabs.
  • Statuses set by formulas or forms need the schedule (or a manual move).
  • Open the Log for the last run and any message.

"Only the automation owner can…"

Someone else saved the setup first. Ask the owner, or take over if they've left the file.

A column is empty in the destination

The destination has no column with that header name, or the source cell was empty. Add a column with the same header.

Emails didn't arrive or have no signature

Send yourself a test, check spam, check the recipient column has valid addresses, check Add my signature is on for that notification, and look for a quota message in the Log.

The plan still says Free after buying

Click Already bought? on the Move tab. Make sure you bought with the same Google account you use in Sheets. Still stuck? Email support with your purchase email.

16. Turn off or uninstall

To stop automation in one spreadsheet, turn off Move instantly and Run on a schedule in Behavior and save. To remove Tab Transfer completely, go to Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, open Tab Transfer's menu and choose Uninstall. You can also remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Attachment files stay in your Drive folder until you delete them.
