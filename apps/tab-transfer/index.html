A1 Features

Everything a "move this row" macro should have done.

Copy-paste scripts lose formatting, break formulas and trip over shared sheets. Tab Transfer was built to handle the details.

Status-based rules

Map each status to a destination tab: Approved → Approved, Done → Archive. Import statuses straight from your dropdown or selected cells, and auto-match them to tabs with the same name.

Formulas stay intact

Per-row formulas in the destination are extended to new rows with references adjusted. ARRAYFORMULA, BYROW, QUERY and FILTER columns (even formulas in the header cell) are left alone, so nothing breaks with #REF!.

Checkboxes, links & notes

Checkboxes keep their state (custom Yes/No ones too), dropdown chips stay chips, and notes, rich-text links and cell formatting travel with the row.

Instant, scheduled or on demand

Rows move the moment a status is changed, on a schedule (every 1 to 12 hours or daily at a set time), or in a batch whenever you click Move rows.

Email notifications

Send an email per moved row or one digest per run, with {{Any Header}} placeholders, recipients from a column, CC, reply-to, your Gmail signature and file attachments.

Documents from templates

Turn each moved row into a filled-in Google Doc or PDF: invoices, contracts, certificates. Use a Google Doc or Word template with {{Header}} placeholders, or fill the fields of a PDF form. The link lands in the row and the PDF can ride along in the email.

Safe for shared sheets

One owner sets it up; editors just change statuses. Every run is recorded in an activity log, and editors can take over if the owner leaves the file.

Column check

One click compares the headers of every source and destination tab. Columns that would be lost, near-miss names like E-mail vs Email, and missing tabs are flagged before you save.

Big batches finish themselves

Thousands of rows? Tab Transfer works to a time budget, stops cleanly before Google's 6-minute limit and continues a minute later, so nothing is half-moved or emailed twice.

B1 How it works

Set up in about two minutes.

  1. Install

    Install Tab Transfer from the Google Workspace Marketplace and approve its permissions.

  2. Open it

    In your spreadsheet go to Extensions → Tab Transfer → Open Tab Transfer.

  3. Map statuses

    Choose your status column, then pick a destination tab for each status.

  4. Save

    Rows now move on their own. Add a schedule, email notifications or documents any time.

D1 Under the hood

Careful with your sheet.

Tab Transfer reads and writes in bulk, so it stays fast even when thousands of rows move at once.

  • Columns matched by header name, so tabs don't need the same layout
  • Column check warns before a missing column would lose data
  • Per-row destination formulas are extended; spilling formulas are left alone
  • Conditional-formatting rules are never duplicated into the destination
  • Choose to delete the source row or just clear it
  • Keep the row's own look, match the destination tab, or skip formatting
  • Optional "moved on" timestamp column
  • Exclude tabs you never want scanned
  • Moved rows are removed in grouped blocks, fast even for thousands of rows
  • Huge batches pause before Google's 6-minute limit and finish on their own a minute later
  • Pre-filled formula and checkbox rows below your data are filled first, not skipped

F1 FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Tab Transfer free?

Yes. The free plan has no time limit and includes three status rules, instant moves and manual moves. Pro ($18.99, one-time) adds unlimited rules, scheduled runs and email notifications with attachments. Ultra ($26.99, one-time) adds documents from templates on top. Both cover every spreadsheet you automate.

Are Pro and Ultra subscriptions?

No. You pay once. The license is tied to the Google account you buy it with, so it works in every spreadsheet that account automates and comes back automatically if you reinstall. Editors who just change statuses in your sheet don't need a license.

Do the tabs need identical columns?

No. Tab Transfer matches columns by header name (case-insensitive), so tabs can have their columns in different orders, and a destination can have extra columns of its own, for example a formula column or a "Moved on" timestamp. Columns that don't exist in the destination are skipped, and the Column check in the Rules tab lists them before anything is lost.

What happens to formulas in the row being moved?

Formulas from the source row arrive as their calculated values, because their cell references wouldn't be valid in another tab. Formulas that already exist in the destination tab are never overwritten. Per-row formulas are extended to the new rows automatically.

Who can set it up?

The first person to save the setup in a spreadsheet becomes its owner. Only the owner can change rules or run batch moves; editors can still change statuses, and the owner's automation moves those rows for them. If the owner loses access to the file, an editor can take over.

Will it send emails from my account?

Only if you turn email notifications on. Emails are sent with your own Google account through Google's mail service and count toward your daily Google sending quota. You can send yourself a test email before saving.

Can emails include attachments?

Yes, with Pro or Ultra. Attach up to 5 files (15 MB each, 20 MB in total) to any notification. They're stored in a Tab Transfer attachments folder in your own Google Drive, and the add-on can only access the files it stored there.

Can it create a document or PDF for each row?

Yes, with Ultra. Choose a Google Doc or Word template with {{Header}} placeholders, or a PDF with form fields, for any destination tab. Each moved row gets its own file (a PDF, a Google Doc, or a flattened or fillable PDF form) saved in your Drive, with a link written into the row. The PDF can also be attached to that row's email.

Does it work with very large sheets?

Yes. Rows are read, written and deleted in bulk. If a huge batch would run past Google's 6-minute limit for add-ons, Tab Transfer pauses at 5 minutes, then continues automatically about a minute later until everything has moved, without duplicating rows or emails.

Can I undo a move?

Moves can be reversed with Sheets' version history (File → Version history). For extra safety you can choose to clear the source row instead of deleting it, and try the setup on a copy of your spreadsheet first.

Does Tab Transfer see or store my spreadsheet data elsewhere?

No. It runs entirely inside Google Apps Script under your account. Its settings are saved with the spreadsheet itself, and no spreadsheet content is sent to macrotool or any third party. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get help?

Click the ? next to any heading in the add-on, open Extensions → Tab Transfer → Help & guide, read the guide, or email support@macrotool.app.

Stuck on something?

The guide covers every option. If you still need help, email support and you'll hear back from the developer directly.
