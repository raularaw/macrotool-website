C1 Quick answers

Common questions

The Tab Transfer menu isn't showing

Reload the spreadsheet after installing. If it's still missing, check Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons to confirm it's installed for the account you're using in that browser tab.

How do I uninstall an app?

In Google Sheets go to Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, open the app's menu and choose Uninstall. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Is my data safe?

The apps run inside Google Apps Script under your account and don't send your content to our servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Can my admin install it for the whole organisation?

Yes. Google Workspace administrators can install Marketplace add-ons for users or organisational units from the Admin console.
