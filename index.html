C1 How we build

Made the way you'd want a tool inside your data to be made.

01 /

Native to Workspace

Apps live in the Extensions menu of the Google apps you already use. No new logins, no separate dashboards, nothing to export.

02 /

Your data stays yours

Each app asks only for the permissions it needs, and keeps its settings with your own file. We don't run servers that copy or store your content.

03 /

Built by a practitioner

Designed by a developer who builds custom Workspace automations for businesses every day — so the edge cases are already handled.
